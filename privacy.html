Privacy Notice

Simple by design: the HRTechify website does not collect or store personal information.

The short version

You can browse HRTechify without creating an account, filling in a form, accepting analytics cookies or giving this website personal information.

What this website does not collect

The HRTechify master-brand website does not intentionally collect, save, sell, rent, profile or share personal information about visitors.

  • No user accounts.
  • No contact-form submissions.
  • No newsletter sign-up on this site.
  • No advertising trackers.
  • No third-party analytics service enabled by this site.
  • No website database containing names, email addresses or messages.

When you want to reach out

HRTechify does not ask you to enter your name, email address or message into a website form. The Contact page offers a direct email link instead. Clicking it opens your own email application; the HRTechify website itself receives and stores nothing from that action.

If you choose to send an email, the email is handled by your email provider and HRTechify’s email provider, not by this website. The message may remain in those mail systems as part of the conversation. HRTechify does not copy that information into a website database, CRM or marketing list through this site.

Cookies and browser storage

This site does not use cookies or browser storage to build a profile of you, remember marketing preferences or track you across websites.

Links to product sites and other services

CorporateX and GrowWithHR are separate product destinations. If you choose to visit them, their own privacy practices apply. The same is true for any third-party service you intentionally open from HRTechify.

Changes

If HRTechify later adds a feature that collects personal information, this notice should be updated before that feature is enabled so the change is clear to visitors.
