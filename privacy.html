Privacy Notice

Draft implementation notice — review before production launch.

Privacy by design

The HRTechify master-brand site is designed to collect as little personal information as practical. No third-party analytics service is enabled in this repository by default.

Contact enquiries

The contact form interface is included, but no live submission endpoint is configured. Before launch, HRTechify should connect a trusted server-side or form provider, add spam protection, record explicit consent, and publish the data-retention details for that provider.

Product links

Links to CorporateX and GrowWith HR lead to separate product destinations. Their privacy practices should be explained on those product sites.

Research and compliance information

The HRTechify brand site should not collect unnecessary employee data or compliance facts. Product-specific information belongs in the relevant product environment under its own controls.

Updates

This draft should be reviewed and replaced with an approved corporate privacy notice before production launch.
