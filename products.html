What We Build

Two product directions. One connected HR point of view.

HRTechify is developing products for both sides of work: the people experiencing it and the organisations designing the systems around it.

Current stageThese products are actively evolving. HRTechify is not yet presented as a full-fledged operating company.
01

For people

CorporateX

CorporateX is being built to give candidates, employees and former employees a more structured way to understand workplace reality. It focuses on context, experience and the reasons people stay, leave or look back differently on a workplace.

02

For HR & organisations

GrowWithHR

GrowWithHR is being developed as an HR and organisational intelligence layer for founders, HR professionals and organisations navigating people practices, policy questions, compliance obligations and organisational complexity.

Boundary: GrowWithHR is not legal advice, legal certification or proof of compliance. Where specialist review is appropriate, the product should say so clearly.

How they connect

People experience and organisational systems are two views of the same workplace.

The longer-term HRTechify ambition is to connect these perspectives more deeply. That is a direction of travel, not a claim that a fully integrated platform already exists.

Questions buyers and collaborators may ask

Clear boundaries matter.

Are both products commercially mature today?

No. They are active product directions at different stages of development and iteration.

Will HRTechify add more products?

Possibly, but the priority is to strengthen the current HR product directions rather than appear broader than the underlying capability.

Can an organisation collaborate on testing or feedback?

Yes. Product feedback, practical HR input and carefully scoped collaboration are welcome.
