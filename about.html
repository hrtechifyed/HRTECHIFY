Why HRTechify exists

People and organisations are often given different versions of the same workplace.

Many HR tools optimise for organisational process. Many employee-facing platforms optimise for ratings or reaction. I am exploring what becomes possible when workplace experience and organisational systems are understood together.

Operating principles

What I believe. What I am trying to build.

VISION

Connected growth

People, technology and organisational growth should reinforce one another rather than compete.

MISSION

HR as an enabler

Help make HR more useful to both people and organisations through clearer systems and better decisions.

PHILOSOPHY

Evidence + empathy

Good HR judgement needs explainable information without losing the human context behind it.

CURRENT STAGE

Build before scale

Strengthen the idea, products and usefulness before making claims about scale or maturity.

Anurag Sinha — Founder

Building the idea one useful piece at a time.

HRTechify is an idea I am actively developing. I have spent years working in HR and kept returning to the same gap: organisations and people are rarely given tools that serve both sides honestly.

That belief shapes what sits under HRTechify today — from GrowWithHR, which helps organisations make sense of people obligations and organisational complexity, to CorporateX, which gives people a more structured way to share and understand workplace reality.

The priority right now is to build useful things, learn openly, improve the products and speak with people who care about the same problems.

I welcome thoughtful collaboration, product feedback, partnership conversations and criticism that makes the work better.
