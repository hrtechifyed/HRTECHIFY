About HRTechify

Why HRTechify exists

Work is becoming more complex. Employees need better context before career decisions. Organizations need better systems for navigating people, technology and compliance. HRTechify exists to make those decisions clearer.

Our framework

People • Technology • Growth

People

Understand workplace reality, leadership, careers, trust and the moments that shape human decisions.

Technology

Use responsible AI, structured intelligence and explainable systems to make complexity clearer.

Growth

Turn understanding into stronger capability, better organizations and sustainable progress.

What we build

Intelligence for both sides of work.

CorporateX by HRTechify

The employee-side lens: structured workplace stories and exit journeys that help professionals understand what changed, why people left and what to ask before making a career decision.

GrowWith HR by HRTechify

The organization-side lens: structured HR compliance intelligence that applies deterministic rules to organizational facts and uses governed sources and AI for explanation.

What we believe

Five operating principles.

  1. 01Context before judgment.
  2. 02Evidence before conclusions.
  3. 03Technology should explain decisions — not hide them.
  4. 04Employee reality deserves structure, not noise.
  5. 05Good organizations improve by understanding both people and systems.

Founder — Anurag Sinha

A career across technology and people.

Anurag Sinha is the founder of HRTechify, a workplace-technology brand built around People • Technology • Growth. His career began with an engineering and technology foundation and later moved into Human Resources, talent and organizational development. Across roles connected with Flipkart, Amazon and Siemens, he has worked at the intersection of people strategy, leadership, organizational development and technology.

That cross-functional path shapes HRTechify’s philosophy: understand what people actually experience, use technology responsibly, and turn insight into better decisions and sustainable growth.
