About Anurag

HRTechify isn’t a finished company — it’s an idea I’m actively building, one piece at a time. I’ve spent some years in the HR world, and yet it still feels like I’m just starting. Even so, I kept running into the same gap: organizations and people are rarely given tools that serve both sides honestly. Most HR technology optimizes for the organization. Most employee-facing tools optimize for venting or ratings. I wanted to build something that treats people and systems as equally important — not a trade-off.

That belief shapes everything under HRTechify so far — from GrowWithHR, which helps organizations understand their compliance and people obligations more clearly, to CorporateX, which gives people a more honest way to share what a workplace was actually like.

This isn’t about monetizing right now. It’s about building the idea, getting it right, and learning from the people who engage with it. There’s a long way to go, and I know it. I’m building this in the open, and I’d genuinely welcome collaboration, partnership, or advice from anyone who’s thought seriously about people, organizations, or technology — especially if you see a gap I’m missing.
