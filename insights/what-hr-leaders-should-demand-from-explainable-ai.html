What HR leaders should demand from explainable AI

Explainability should be designed into an HR system, not added as a marketing label after the decision is made.

HRTechify Insight • Founding perspective

Trace the path

A user should be able to see which facts mattered, which rule or policy path was applied, and which source supported the result.

Make source quality visible

Governed sources need ownership, versioning and a clear relationship to the rule being applied. “The AI said so” is not a source.

Keep roles explicit

The system should make clear what is deterministic, what is retrieved evidence, what is generated explanation, and where a human remains accountable.

Prefer responsible boundaries

A credible product states what it cannot guarantee. Explainable AI should make uncertainty and product boundaries clearer rather than hide them behind confident language.

Editorial principle

Context before judgment. Evidence before conclusions.
