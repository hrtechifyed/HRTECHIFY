From the founder

Working on it one piece at a time, in the open.

HRTechify began with a recurring HR problem: organisations and people are often given different tools, different information and different versions of the same workplace reality. I am trying to create useful bridges between those perspectives without pretending the work is already finished.

That is why I want HRTechify to be deliberately transparent about its current stage. It is an initiative under active development, with products, experiments and published thinking evolving as the idea becomes stronger.

Questions, briefly answered

What HRTechify is — and is not.

Is HRTechify already a full-fledged company?

No. HRTechify is currently a founder-built initiative in active development. The website presents what I am working on and exploring without claiming a scale, operating model or commercial maturity that does not yet exist.

What am I working on through HRTechify?

Today the main product directions are CorporateX and GrowWithHR, supported by my practical views on talent, workplace realities, leadership and responsible technology.

Is GrowWithHR legal or compliance advice?

No. It is an HR and organisational intelligence product direction. It should not be treated as legal advice, certification or proof of compliance.

Can I collaborate with HRTechify?

Yes. Thoughtful product feedback, HR collaboration, research conversations and partnership ideas are welcome.
